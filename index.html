Zoo Boom - Match 3 Puzzle Adventure

A colorful match-3 puzzle game where you help cute animals by matching tiles and solving challenging levels.

How to Play Zoo Boom

Zoo Boom is an exciting match-3 puzzle game where you help cute animals by matching colorful tiles. Swap adjacent tiles to create matches of three or more identical items to clear them from the board and score points.

Game Controls

  • Click or tap and drag to swap tiles
  • Match 3 or more identical tiles to clear them
  • Create special tiles by matching 4 or more in a row
  • Use power-ups to clear difficult sections

Game Objectives

Each level has different objectives to complete, such as:

  • Reach a target score within a limited number of moves
  • Clear specific tiles or obstacles from the board
  • Collect special items by matching tiles next to them
  • Help animals by completing themed challenges

Game Features

Power-Ups

Create special tiles and power-ups by matching 4 or more tiles in various patterns.

Hundreds of Levels

Progress through hundreds of increasingly challenging levels with unique objectives.

Meet adorable animal characters as you progress through the zoo-themed adventure.

Tips & Strategies

  • Focus on objectives: Always prioritize the level objectives rather than just making random matches.

  • Create special tiles: Try to create special tiles by matching 4 or more in a row, or in L or T shapes.

  • Combine power-ups: Swap two special tiles together for powerful combo effects that can clear large sections of the board.

  • Plan ahead: Look at the entire board before making a move to identify the most strategic matches.
